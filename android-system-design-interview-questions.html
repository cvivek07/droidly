Android System Design Interview Questions

Senior Android interviews almost always include a system design round: design the Android client for a chat app, a feed, a payment flow or an image loader. These walkthroughs cover how to approach each one, from requirements to architecture, offline support and the edge cases interviewers probe.

By Vivek Choudhary, Android engineer · Updated October 2026 · How answers are checked

How to answer an Android system design question

  1. Clarify the scopeAsk which screens and flows matter, who the users are, and whether you are designing the Android client, the backend, or both. Write down 3–5 functional requirements.
  2. Agree on the constraintsOffline support, network quality, battery, memory, app size, privacy and scale. On mobile these drive most of the design.
  3. Sketch the high-level designLayers (UI, ViewModel, repository, local and remote data sources), how data flows between them, and which component owns the source of truth.
  4. Go deep on the hard partsPick the 2–3 problems that make this app difficult: sync and conflicts, pagination, caching, real-time updates, background work, or process death.
  5. Talk about trade-offs and edge casesExplain what you chose and what you gave up, then cover failures: no network, slow network, app killed, duplicate requests, and how you would test and monitor it.
